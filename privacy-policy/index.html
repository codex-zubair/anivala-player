AniLoom

Offline Anime & Video Player · codex-zubair

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Overview

AniLoom (“the App”) is a free, ad-free, fully offline local media player for Android and Android TV, published by codex-zubair (“we”, “us”). AniLoom scans and plays video files that are already stored on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect when you use it. By using AniLoom you agree to this policy.

In one sentence: AniLoom collects no personal data, contains no advertising or analytics, requests no internet permission, and never connects to the internet.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No account, sign-in, or registration is required.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts are collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses are collected.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools are used.
  • We do not know which videos you have on your device or what you watch.

3. Internet Access

AniLoom does not request or use the Android INTERNET permission. The App is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. It cannot stream, download, upload, or communicate with any server. All scanning, playback and bookkeeping happen entirely on your device. This makes AniLoom suitable for privacy-sensitive users and for offline or air-gapped use.

4. Local Files and Permissions

To build your library, AniLoom reads video files that are already on your device. On Android 13 and later it requests only the scoped READ_MEDIA_VIDEO permission; on Android 12 and earlier it requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. AniLoom does not request broad “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

  • The App only reads media; it never modifies or deletes your files.
  • File names and folder names are read to display your library. They stay on your device.
  • You can revoke these permissions at any time in Android Settings; the App will then simply show an empty library.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

AniLoom keeps a small amount of data locally so it can work offline:

  • A local index of the video files it found (title, folder, path, size, duration).
  • Your Continue Watching positions — the playback time you reached in each video — so it can resume where you left off.
  • Simple preferences, such as your preferred grid or list layout.

All of this is stored in the App's private storage on your device using the operating system's preferences store. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Clearing the data in the App's Settings screen, or uninstalling the App, permanently deletes it.

6. Permissions

AniLoom requests only media read access, as described in Section 4. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, or network. The optional links on the About screen (GitHub and this Privacy Policy) open your web browser only when you explicitly tap them.

7. Third Parties

The App contains no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The App uses the open-source video_player plugin to play your local files, and url_launcher solely to open links you explicitly tap. No data is shared with anyone.

8. Children's Privacy

AniLoom is a general-purpose local media player suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else. You are responsible for the media files you choose to store and play.

9. Data Security

Since no data leaves your device and none is transmitted, there is no data for us to secure or for a third party to breach. Your files and history remain under your control on your own device.

10. Open Source

AniLoom's complete source code is public at github.com/codex-zubair/anivala-player. Anyone may inspect the code to verify these privacy claims.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in a future app update and published on this page.
