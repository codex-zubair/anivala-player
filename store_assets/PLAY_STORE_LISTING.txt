--- Full description (max 4000) ---
AniLoom is a fast, clean, fully offline video player for your own anime and
animation collection on Android phones, tablets and Android TV. It scans the
video files already stored on your device and plays them with a smooth,
distraction-free player. No internet connection is ever required.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
AniLoom requests no INTERNET permission at all. It is technically incapable
of connecting to the network, so your library and viewing habits can never
leave your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

CONTINUE WATCHING
AniLoom remembers exactly where you stopped in every video and shows a
"Continue Watching" dashboard the moment you open the app. Tap any card to
resume from the precise second you left off. Positions are saved locally and
automatically.

YOUR LOCAL LIBRARY
- Scans your device with Android's scoped media permission (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO).
- Groups videos by folder so series stay together.
- Search your whole library instantly.
- Grid or list view, whichever you prefer.
- Supports MP4, MKV, WEBM, AVI, MOV, M4V, TS, FLV, WMV, MPEG and more.

A PLAYER BUILT FOR WATCHING
- Smooth hardware-accelerated playback.
- Built-in gesture controls and a clean touchseek bar.
- Skip back 10 seconds or forward 10 seconds with a double tap.
- Auto-hiding controls for a full-screen experience.
- Landscape and portrait support.

MADE FOR ANDROID TV
- Android TV launcher support with a dedicated TV banner.
- Works without a touchscreen, driven by the D-pad remote.

PRIVACY AT A GLANCE
- No internet permission, no ads, no analytics.
- Only reads your media; it never modifies or deletes your files.
- Library index, Continue Watching history and preferences are stored only
  in the app's private storage on your device.
- Clear your data any time from the in-app Settings screen, or by
  uninstalling the app.

Read the full privacy policy at the link on this listing.

Open source

PERMISSIONS DECLARATION (Play Console -> App content -> sensitive perms)
========================================================================
AniLoom declares ONLY these Android permissions:
  - android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO     (Android 13+; read local videos)
  - android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below; media read)
  - android.permission.WAKE_LOCK            (keep screen on during playback)

It explicitly REMOVES any network permission:
  - android.permission.INTERNET           (tools:node="remove")
  - android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (tools:node="remove")

There is NO location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, or AD_ID
permission, and NO MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access"). Because of
this the Play Console will not require an "All files access" declaration and
the app qualifies for the "No data collected" data safety label.
