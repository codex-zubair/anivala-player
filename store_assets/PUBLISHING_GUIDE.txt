7. SET UP RELEASE SIGNING (Play App Signing) [10 minutes]
========================================================================
Google recommends "Play App Signing".

  OPTION A (default, recommended): Let Google manage the app signing key.
    - When you upload your first AAB, Play will offer to enroll in Play App
      Signing.
    - You upload the AAB signed with YOUR upload key
      (upload-keystore.jks from ALL-APP-KEYS/AniLoom).
    - Google stores the real app signing key; your JKS becomes the "upload
      key". You keep building with the SAME upload key forever.
    - If your upload key is ever lost, you can request a reset from Google.

  OPTION B: Export & upload your own signing key manually. Not recommended
    for a first release.

ACTION: When prompted, choose "Continue" to enroll in Play App Signing and
upload your AAB signed with the existing upload-keystore.jks.

IMPORTANT: The keystore in /mnt/external_disk/ALL-APP-KEYS/AniLoom is your
upload key. Back it up (encrypted USB / password manager). Losing it is
recoverable with Play App Signing, but the process takes support time.

14. TROUBLESHOOTING
========================================================================
- "App bundle was not signed" -> rebuild with the keystore present in
  android/key.properties; do not commit it, just have it locally.
- "Version code already used" -> increase the +N in pubspec version.
- "Privacy policy URL invalid" -> the URL above returns 200; re-check if you
  changed the repo.
- "Data safety mismatch" -> you selected a data type the app does not
  collect; re-answer per Section 6.
- "compileSdk" build error -> the project pins compileSdk = 37 (required by
  permission_handler_android). Android SDK 37 must be installed.
- Screenshot rejected -> must be PNG/JPEG, 16:9-ish (max 2:1), min 320px.
  The provided 1080x1920 PNGs are accepted.
- Feature graphic rejected -> exactly 1024x500, PNG/JPEG, no transparency.
  Provided file complies.
